# NivCraft Account and Data Deletion

NivCraft users can request deletion of their app account and associated data by either method below.

## Delete in the app

1. Sign in to NivCraft.
2. Open **Menu**.
3. Select **Delete Account** below **Logout**.
4. Review the notice and confirm deletion.

The app signs out on the device after the deletion request completes.

## Request deletion without the app

Email **support.nivcraft@gmail.com** from the email address associated with your NivCraft account. Use the subject **NivCraft account deletion request** and state that you want your NivCraft account and associated data deleted.

We may ask for limited information to verify that you control the account. Do not send a password, one-time password, broker token, card number, or bank credential.

## What is deleted and what may be retained

After verification, NivCraft deletes or de-identifies profile, watchlist, paper-trading history synchronized for ranking, Community, support-chat, push-device, device-session, and similar user-specific data held by NivCraft. Authored Community items and their child comments, votes, mentions, and queued notifications are removed together. Recorded Terms acceptance is cleared. If you later return, you must accept the then-current Terms again.

The app also clears its active local session and local account data for the deleted account. Data stored in device or cloud backups outside NivCraft's control may remain until you remove it or the backup rotates.

NivCraft may retain only restricted records needed for a stated lawful purpose: a one-way identity fingerprint, membership and entitlement tier, premium expiry, payment and renewal ledger, referral/reward ledger, deletion record, and records required for fraud prevention, disputes, tax, accounting, or legal compliance. This prevents repeated welcome offers or rewards and preserves legitimate billing history without retaining your deleted Community, support, watchlist, wallet-balance, order, position, or practice-trading content. These records are removed or de-identified when their purpose and mandatory retention period end. Backups may remain until their limited rotation period ends. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for more detail.

We will normally complete a verified request within 30 days, or explain if applicable law permits or requires additional time.
